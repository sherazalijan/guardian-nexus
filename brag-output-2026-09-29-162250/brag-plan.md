# /brag Plan: Guardian Nexus

## Strategy
**Angle:** Emphasize the real-time visual spectacle of detecting a scam mid-sentence.
**Hook:** A glowing radar dial hitting critical mass.
**Highlights:** 
1. Starting the session ("Guardian Active")
2. The waveform reacting to speech.
3. The Scam Alert Banner hazard stripes slamming down.
**Punchline:** "Guardian Nexus. Powered by AssemblyAI."
**Tone:** `cinematic` - Big motion, serious, dramatic.
**Visual Identity:**
- Background: `#f8fafc` with a dotted radial glow grid (`.nx-canvas`)
- Accent: `#2563EB` (Primary), `#E11D48` (Critical Rose for alert)
- Display Font: `Inter`

## Storyboard (20s)

**Scene 1: The Hook (0-4s)**
- *Visual:* Macro zoom on the `LiveSentinel` 270° gauge at `0` (Emerald). The radar sweep pulses.
- *Action:* A glowing cursor clicks the blue "Start Session" button.
- *Audio:* Deep cinematic bass drop.

**Scene 2: The Analysis (4-10s)**
- *Visual:* Pull back to wide layout. The live transcript feeds in: *"Hello, I am calling from your bank..."* 
- *Action:* The waveform bounces dynamically. The gauge smoothly climbs from `0` to `35` (Amber).
- *Audio:* Low ticking synth riser building tension.

**Scene 3: The Result (10-15s)**
- *Visual:* The gauge rips to `85` (Critical). The top of the screen flashes as the `ScamAlertBanner` slams in with diagonal animated hazard stripes.
- *Copy:* "SCAM ATTEMPT DETECTED"
- *Audio:* Heavy impact hit / cinematic alarm chime.

**Scene 4: Outro (15-20s)**
- *Visual:* Fade to the deep indigo gradient (`#1e1b4b`). Center text glows.
- *Copy:* "Guardian Nexus." / "Real-time voice defense."
- *Audio:* Resolving ambient chord fading out.
